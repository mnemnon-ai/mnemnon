> Last updated: 2026-06-09

## What you may do (no permission needed)

- **Refer to Mnemnon truthfully (nominative use).** You may use the name "Mnemnon" to accurately identify the project — e.g., "compatible with Mnemnon," "built on Mnemnon," "an MCP client for Mnemnon" — provided your use does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation.
- **Link to the project** by name.
- **Discuss, review, or teach** Mnemnon in articles, talks, and documentation.

When you do: use the name as an adjective followed by a noun (e.g., "the Mnemnon backend"), keep the spelling and capitalization intact, and include the ™ on first prominent use where practical.

## What requires written permission

- Using "Mnemnon," the logo, or the taglines — **or any confusingly similar variation** — as the name or branding of your own product, service, application, fork, distribution, company, domain, social handle, or package.
- Any use that suggests your offering is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with Mnemnon or Dax Davis.
- Modifying the Marks (altered spellings, logo recoloring or redrawing, combining them with other marks).
- Merchandise or promotional goods bearing the Marks.

## What is not allowed

- Registering domains, social handles, package names, or trademarks that incorporate "Mnemnon" or a confusingly similar term.
- Using the Marks in a way that is misleading or disparaging, or that dilutes their distinctiveness.

## Forks and redistribution

You are free to fork and redistribute the Apache-2.0 code. If you distribute a modified version, **use your own name and branding** — not the Marks — and make clear it is not the official Mnemnon project. You may state, factually, that your work is "based on Mnemnon."

## Requesting permission

For any use beyond nominative reference, request written permission from the owner before using the Marks. Where granted, permission will be in writing and limited to the stated use.
