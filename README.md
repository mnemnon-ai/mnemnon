## What it is

Mnemnon™ (*NEM-non*) is a self-hosted, session-independent memory and knowledge-retrieval backend that serves all of your AI front-ends at once. Instead of each assistant starting from zero — and instead of your memory living on someone else's servers — Mnemnon gives Claude, ChatGPT, and any MCP- or REST-capable client **one shared memory that persists across sessions, tools, and time**, on infrastructure you own.

## What it will do

- **Shared memory across every model.** One memory layer behind Claude, ChatGPT, and any MCP/REST client — not another silo locked to a single assistant.
- **Document RAG over your own corpus.** Retrieval across your personal and work data that surfaces the right passage, with provenance.
- **Graph-aware, long-term recall.** Entities, relationships, and time-aware retrieval — so you get the *current* answer, not just a past one.
- **Self-hosted and fully owned.** Single-tenant, private, yours. Your memory never leaves your infrastructure.
- **Headless by design.** One clean boundary — REST and MCP — so any client can plug in.

## Why

Most "AI memory" is tied to a single assistant and lives in someone else's cloud. Mnemnon takes the opposite position: one memory, every conversation, always yours. Your AIs stop starting over, and your data stays home.
